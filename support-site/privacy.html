VoltLedger Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

VoltLedger is a local-first electric vehicle companion app. It does not require an account, has no login, and operates without its own servers. All of your data — charging records, expenses, vehicle photos, and preferences — is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created by you and stored only on your device:

  • Charging records (cost, energy, location, date)
  • Maintenance and ownership expense records
  • Vehicle profiles and photos
  • Inspection, maintenance, and insurance memos
  • Calculator inputs and app preferences (theme, drafts)
  • Optional backups you export as files

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use one of the features described in Sections 3–5, or export a backup yourself.

3. Permissions We Use

  • Location — used to auto-fill the location of a new charging record and to find charging stations near you. Your current location is used only while these features are active and is not saved unless you save a record.
  • Camera & Photo Library — used to scan charging receipts with OCR and to attach vehicle photos. Images for OCR are processed as described in Section 4; vehicle photos never leave your device.

4. Third-Party Services

A few optional features rely on third-party services. When you use them, the following data is sent:

  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API — receives your approximate coordinates to search for nearby charging stations. No other data is sent.
  • AI Receipt Recognition (OCR) — when you scan a receipt, the image is sent to an AI vision service to extract charge details. This feature may be unavailable in some regions. If you prefer not to send images, you can always enter records manually.
  • External shopping links — links to recommended EV accessories open in your device's browser. Those websites are governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Maps — charging spot maps are rendered using Apple Maps on your device.

5. Purchases

VoltLedger offers an optional subscription (VoltLedger Pro). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Our subscription analytics provider (RevenueCat) receives anonymous transaction information (such as subscription status and purchase date) to deliver the features you paid for. We do not receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID.

6. Data Deletion

Because everything is stored on your device, deleting the VoltLedger app permanently deletes all of your data. Backup files you exported are stored wherever you saved them and are managed by you.

7. Children

VoltLedger is a utility app and is not directed at children under 13. We do knowingly collect no data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at fishspotradar@163.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored? Is it uploaded anywhere?

All of your data lives only on your device — VoltLedger has no servers and no login. See our Privacy Policy for details on the few optional features that contact third-party services (nearby stations, receipt OCR).

How do I back up or restore my data?

Go to Profile → Backup & Restore. Export creates a JSON backup file that you can save to Files, iCloud Drive, or anywhere you like. Import reads a backup file back and merges it with your existing data without creating duplicates.

The receipt OCR option doesn't appear. Why?

Receipt recognition is an optional feature and may be unavailable in some regions or configurations. You can always add charging records manually — OCR is only a shortcut.

How do I cancel my VoltLedger Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions (or Settings → App Store → your Apple ID → View Apple ID → Subscriptions), select VoltLedger Pro, and cancel. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Automatic location or nearby stations isn't working.

Check that VoltLedger has permission to use your location: iPhone Settings → VoltLedger → Location. Nearby stations also need an internet connection. If the station service is temporarily busy, try again in a few minutes.
